health journal (`--health-log`, one JSON line every 5 s) and are reproducible with:

## 2. Chaos: silent stream and dropped connection (recorded in the demo video)

The guard ran through `scripts/chaos_proxy.py` (TLS passthrough to `grpc.solami.dev:443`) with
`FALLBACK_SILENCE_SEC=15`, `STREAM_STALE_SEC=15`; Telegram alerts live.

| Step | What the guard did (from its log) |
|---|---|
| `pause` — bytes stop, TLS stays open | after **15 s** of silence: `fallback_on` — RPC polling every 2 s took over; `stream_stale` and `slot_lag` (57 slots behind) raised; the panel path pill turned `rpc-fallback` |
| `resume` | `fallback_off` — *grpc stream is back after 7 s (4 RPC polls covered the gap)*; path back to `grpc` |
| `drop` — connection closed | `UNAVAILABLE: Stream removed` → reconnect in **~1 s** → `replaying 3 slots via from_slot` → stream healthy |

Nothing was skipped and the guard never went blind: during the pause the account set kept updating over RPC, and the
reconnect resumed from the last seen slot instead of the head.
